What we updated

Home page

  • The main headline now reads more like a sentence — "Technology Investing" sits on its own line and "Investing" is no longer in italics. The page feels more institutional at first glance.
  • The dark navy wash behind the hero video is a touch deeper, which makes the white headline pop more clearly.
  • The portfolio logo carousel is in your specified order: Axim Geospatial → CBEYONData → CTI → Precise Systems → Qualis → InTrack Radar Technologies → Tektonux → Valiant Solutions → Abile Group.
  • The carousel logos now appear cleanly as they slide into view (before, they would briefly "pop in" as they entered the screen — that's been fixed by loading them all up front).
  • The carousel is now draggable — visitors can click-and-drag with a mouse or swipe on a phone to scroll through the logos manually. It still auto-scrolls when left alone.
  • Hovering directly over "Learn More" on a carousel card now shows the same animated underline + arrow movement that appears on the Investments page — feels consistent across the site.
  • The logo sizing in the carousel is balanced — bigger logos no longer dwarf smaller ones.

About page

  • The hero headline now reads cleanly across two lines: "Creating Value / Through Partnership." No italics.
  • The dark navy wash on the About hero is lighter than the Home page, so the photograph stays visible while the headline remains readable.
  • "Our Firm" section has a new split layout — copy on the left, a navy parallelogram graphic on the right — giving the section more visual structure.
  • "Empowering Management Teams" has been renamed to "Empowering Leadership Teams," and the supporting paragraph is now in the larger body style for easier reading.
  • The Approach cards no longer show a clickable cursor on hover (they weren't meant to be clickable).

Team page

  • The subtitle under the page header has been removed for a cleaner introduction.
  • The bio pop-up now has an email icon alongside the LinkedIn icon. Once you provide email addresses per team member, tapping the icon will open the visitor's email app pre-addressed to that person.

Investments page

  • All 27 companies are now in your preferred order: PAI in position 1, followed by Valiant Solutions, Abile Group, Qualis, Tektonux, and the rest of the sequence you sent through.
  • The new CTI logo has been swapped in across the site.
  • The new PAI logo has been added and tuned so it stays clearly visible in the default (muted) state — the original light-blue gradient was getting washed out, which we've corrected without changing the brand's look.
  • Logo size in each card has been reduced about 15% so the marks breathe inside the card frame rather than crowding it.
  • Card heights have been normalized so the grid looks even across mobile and desktop.
  • "Investment Criteria" rows: the icons now line up vertically with the text next to them, and bullet markers have been removed for a more polished, editorial look.
  • The filter pills are centered on smaller screens and left-aligned on desktop.

News (article detail page)

  • The article detail page has been simplified: just the publication date, the headline, a short summary, and a clear "View Article" button that opens the full article on the original source in a new browser tab. The category tag and "min read" estimate have been removed.

Site-wide polish

  • The mobile menu button (hamburger) now stays perfectly aligned next to the BLUESTONE logo in every state — at first load over the hero, after scrolling (where the bar shrinks), and when the navy menu drawer is open. Previously it drifted between states.
  • Entrance animations across the whole site have been simplified — text now fades up as a single paragraph rather than revealing word-by-word. The new motion feels more institutional and is easier to read.
  • The footer phone number is now a tap-to-call link, and the email address opens the visitor's mail app — handy for mobile users.
  • The footer LinkedIn icon links to the live Bluestone Investment Partners LinkedIn company page.
  • News articles can now optionally link out to an external source (used by the "View Article" button mentioned above).
